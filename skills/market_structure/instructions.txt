Evaluate price structure before interpreting individual indicators. Identify the
visible range, the current price's location, and whether a move beyond a range
has follow-through or is rejected. Distinguish a confirmed close outside the
range from an intrabar wick when the data permits. Treat trend, volume, order
flow, and volatility as evidence only when they are present in the input. Avoid
assuming every breakout continues or every boundary causes a reversal. If the
structure is mixed, late, or unsupported by the supplied candles, choose
no_trade. Never invent swing points or market context.
